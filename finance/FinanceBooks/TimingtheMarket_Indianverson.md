Technical Indicators for the Indian Stock Market1. Trend & Momentum Indicators200-Day Simple Moving Average (SMA):Value to look for: Price staying above the 200-SMA on the daily chart.Indian Context: This is the "Lakshman Rekha" for the Nifty 50; being below it often triggers a long-term bear phase.50-Day & 20-Day Exponential Moving Averages (EMA):Value to look for: A "Golden Cross" where the 20-EMA crosses above the 50-EMA.Relative Strength Index (RSI):Overbought: $> 70$. Look for price/RSI divergence (Price makes a higher high, RSI makes a lower high) as a sell signal.Oversold: $< 30$. Historically, a Nifty RSI of 30 is a high-probability "Buy the Dip" zone.2. Market Breadth & SentimentAdvance-Decline (A/D) Ratio:Value to look for: A ratio of $2:1$ or higher (two stocks rising for every one falling).Significance: Confirms a "healthy" rally. If Nifty rises while the ratio is below $1:1$, the move is likely narrow and unstable.India VIX (Volatility Index):Low Range ($10–13$): Indicates market complacency; be cautious of a sudden sharp correction.High Range ($18–25$): Indicates extreme fear. In India, a VIX near 25 often signals a market bottom.3. Support, Resistance & RetracementsFibonacci Retracements:Value to look for: Pullbacks to the $50\%$ or $61.8\%$ levels.Strategy: We look for bullish candlestick patterns (like a Hammer) exactly at these percentage levels during a Nifty correction.Pivot Points (Standard):Value to look for: R2/R3 for Resistance and S2/S3 for Support.Indian Context: These levels are mathematically calculated daily and are highly respected by intraday traders on the NSE.4. Derivative & Institutional IndicatorsPut-Call Ratio (PCR):Overbought (Bearish reversal): $> 1.4$ or $1.5$. Suggests excessive bullishness.Oversold (Bullish reversal): $< 0.7$ or $0.6$. Suggests the market is "short" and a short-covering rally is likely.On-Balance Volume (OBV) & FII Flows:Value to look for: Rising OBV combined with consecutive days of Net Buying by Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs).Significance: This signals "Smart Money" accumulation even if the price appears sideways.5. Market CyclesHistorical Cycles:Election Cycle: Tracking the 4-year cycle leading up to Indian General Elections.Seasonal Cycles: Monitoring the "Budget Rally" in January/February and the post-monsoon festive season trends.
