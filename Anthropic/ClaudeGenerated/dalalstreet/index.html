CHAPTER 3

Trade on a live order book

The exchange keeps a list of every buy order (bids) and every sell order (asks). A trade happens when a buyer's price meets a seller's. Best price goes first, and at the same price, the earlier order goes first. This is price-time priority. You start with ₹1,00,000 of play money.

    CHAPTER 6

    How a company lists: the IPO

    The primary market is where a company sells new shares to the public. After listing, those shares trade between investors on the secondary market, which is what chapters 2–5 describe. Since December 2023, listing must happen within three working days of the issue closing (T+3).

    MONTHS BEFOREDRHP filedMerchant bankers draft the Draft Red Herring Prospectus and file it with SEBI for review.
    WEEKS BEFOREPrice band setAfter SEBI's observations, the RHP is filed and a price band (e.g. ₹100–105) and lot size are announced.
    T−2 TO TBidding, 3 daysYou bid through ASBA or UPI. Your money is blocked in your bank account, not debited.
    T+1 TO T+2AllotmentThe registrar finalises allotment. Blocked money is debited for allotted shares and released for the rest.
    T+3ListingShares are credited to demat and trading begins after a special pre-open session.

    Who gets shares when an IPO is oversubscribed?

    For a typical mainboard IPO, the issue is split between investor categories:

    QIB 50%
    NII 15%
    Retail 35%

    QIB: mutual funds, banks, insurers, FPIs. NII: individuals bidding above ₹2 lakh. Retail: up to ₹2 lakh.

    If retail demand exceeds supply, SEBI rules give as many retail applicants as possible one minimum lot each, chosen by lottery. More bids per person don't raise your odds; more applications (from different PANs) do.

    Your chance ≈ 5.0%

    200 retail applicants, each applying for one lot. The orange-outlined dot is you.

    CHAPTER 7

    Circuit breakers: when the market stops

    If the Nifty 50 or Sensex falls (or rises) 10%, 15% or 20% from the previous close, trading halts across all equity and equity-derivative markets nationwide. How long depends on the time of the breach. After a halt, trading restarts with a 15-minute pre-open call auction.
